# OpenSpot 2.3.0

YouTube Music, Plex and your local files in one app.

## New in 2.3.0
- **Pin playlists and folders** to the top of Your Library, like Spotify: right-click (or •••) →
  Pin playlist. Pinned items stay first whatever the sort, with a green pin, up to four. Unpin
  playlist puts it back.
- **Downloads use your YouTube Music account**, so Premium-only songs download too (they failed
  with "only available to Music Premium members").

## New in 2.2.1
- **Downloads are marked like Spotify:** a green arrow next to every downloaded song, a green
  download button on a downloaded playlist or album (press it again to remove the download), and
  the arrow next to downloaded playlists and albums in Your Library. It pulses while songs are
  still downloading.

## New in 2.2.0
- **Download YouTube Music songs** for offline listening, like Plex songs: Download in a song's,
  album's or playlist's menu (or the download button on a playlist or album). They play from disk
  and show under Downloads. Based on [ytspot](https://github.com/Chaitanya-Shahare/ytspot)'s
  downloader.

## New in 2.1.1
- The YTSpot / PLEXSpot import is gone: OpenSpot no longer looks for, removes or imports from the
  retired 1.x apps.

## New in 2.1.0
- **Always show Plex / YouTube Music albums, per artist.** An artist page's ••• menu (next to
  Follow) can keep the other service's albums of that artist on the page, until you turn it off.
  It works even while that service is switched off in Settings (Plex still needs you signed in),
  and those albums play.
- Phones: no empty player bar at the bottom while nothing is playing.

## OpenSpot 2.0
- **YouTube Music and Plex together.** Sign in to either or both. Turn each one on or off in
  Settings → Services.
- **Combined search, Home, Your Library and Liked Songs** across both services.
- **One page per artist.** With both services on, an artist's YouTube Music, Plex and local songs
  and albums are on one page, without duplicates.
- **"Already added" warning** before adding a song a playlist already has.
- **Mixed queues.** Plex and YouTube songs play one after another.
- **F / Ctrl+F** opens Search in playlist.
